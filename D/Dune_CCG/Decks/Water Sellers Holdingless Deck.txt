Deck Strategy:
This is a variation on my first holding-less deck. True, there are holdings in the Imperial deck -- perhaps best to
describe it as holdings-optional in play. And version 1.0 did win in a four player without bringing out a one of
them. There are a couple non-starter rares; none is integral to the deck -- the key cards are fixed (i.e. Water
Peddlers) or uncommon.

Have fun running rites all over the place. Try to get a Water Seller ally out so you won't be relying on your
Homeworld to engage for the win.

Don't bother deploying a holding unless it will pay you back next turn (the zero-cost holdings do this, as will
Water Facilities if Arrakeen is out, and Arsunt just through being a city. But don't refrain from offering a bid)
let your opponents pay to stop you each turn.

Contract Negotiations is a fantastic target for Protracted Duration, suddenly 4 favour is buying you an easy 24+
solaris of aides.

Water Peddlers (especially with Expert Command) convince your rivals not to keep spare solaris lying around --
so you use the Holding Tithes and Enlist Survivors.

With Water Sellers, Guild, and Harkonnen elements, almost any ally counts as adversarial for the easy to fulfil
fall of a Great Rival.
