Strategy:

With Your Best Men About You and Well Equipped Sickbay overcome the general weakness of not being able to tack you skeletal crew for hits. Ideally you want to fill up your ship and start trading shots with your gunners. Black Heart is another card that you can use to go wider than your opponent.

Log of the Black Freighter is really strong if you can play it on turn one, and if you open-hand Reporting Drunk as well then you will start off really far ahead of your opponent.

Be careful, some decks can go over the top of you, so race them to get set up and then switch gears to the offensive while the window is still open.
