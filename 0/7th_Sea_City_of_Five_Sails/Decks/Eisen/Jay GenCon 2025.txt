Biggest differences from Palmer's deck (that was not made in conjunction with him): 1) I had Kaspar's Occupation instead of Marooned as an extra speed plot. I don't think this worked out. I think overall Wilhelm is better, EXCEPT when I really need to kill someone, when Katain with a Matchlock Musket is deadly. So Katain is there for a mid-game pivot killing if I really need him, but most of the time he's my Turn 5 character. 3) Actual deck is mostly the same. It played well in every game.
