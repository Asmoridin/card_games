Suggested first five: Summon Blue Jaguar, Summon Winged Lioness, Immortal Commander, Light Swordsman, and Chained Creations.

The side action costs of the allies paired with the main action costs of the Summons allow you to build up your battlefield incredibly fast. Chained Creations in conjunction with Aradel's ability allow you to keep the opponent's battlefield under control.
Once you have at least mostly dealt with your opponents battlefield you should be able to swing for a large amount of damage.
