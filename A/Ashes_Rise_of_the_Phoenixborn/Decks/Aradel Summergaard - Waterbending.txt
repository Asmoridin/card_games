The focus is the Squall Stallions and combining with explosive growth. Since I'm using Aradel in this version you can also build wide with hunt master and raptor herder and surprise hit with an out of the mist. The rest of the cards are there to support and control the field so the stallions can get through.

My main removal combo is "Deep Freeze" plus "To Shadows" to get rid of any unit since deep freeze considers the unit exhausted.
