This, along with the other “Family of 4” decks, are a good attempt at building four separate “tournament” decks out of the same collection.

First Five suggestion  Frostback Bear, Butterfly, Lioness, Rose Fire Dancer/Dreamlock Mage, Ice Trap

To Shadows or Fade Away can be swapped in for Ice Trap if you expect bigger units.
