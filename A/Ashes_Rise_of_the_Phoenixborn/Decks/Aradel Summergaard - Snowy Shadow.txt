Basic write-up follows, more detailed to possibly come later:

This is an adaptation of KC's Summer Shadow deck that I played in the Winter Open. The goal is essentially to keep afloat long enough until you can sneak through an ExGro Mirror Spirit and then close out with burn or small bypass. Collection performance suggests that this doesn't work, but I still believe that a more refined version of the deck could be solid.

Standard FF: Jaguar (don't summon), Gilder, Frost Frog, Mirror Spirit, Hunt Master, Water Blast, Frog Ping

Alt FF: Jaguar, Frost Frog, Gilder, Mirror Spirit, Duelist, Water Blast
