First attempt at deckbuilding, so this is far from expertly made or refined.. This used the Frostwild Scourge deck as a base, with some support abilities from Aradel's Master Set preconstructed deck. It also has Sleeping Bear because I thought it was funny.

I've been using Shifting Mists, Rejuvenate, Summon Adaptodon, Summon Ice Golem, and Jungle Forager as the First Five, with the goal of quickly fishing up either Will to Live or Wallop for an Ice Golem. Yes, this deck would probably be better with Rin instead of Aradel.

This deck's definitely not competitive-ready, but I'm just starting to wade into the game.
