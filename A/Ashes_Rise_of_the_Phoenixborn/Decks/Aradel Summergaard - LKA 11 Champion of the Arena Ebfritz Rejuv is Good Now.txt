Standard FF: Rejuvenate, Summon Cerulean Diver, Summon Emperor Lion, Summon Shade Prowler, Glory Aspirant->Light Swordsman

This deck aims to keep your opponent's battlefield largely empty of threats. It does this with a large suit of highly effective removal options and units that trade efficiently on the battlefield.

Opposing units with 1-3 health can be killed with various combinations of Diver and any of the many side action ping options in the list. 4 health knights are a slightly harder to kill, but 2 attack conjis + either Water Blast or a charged up Reverb can do well into them.

Prowler and Lion are likely the best rejuvenate targets in the game, with prowler being very good into swarm since each attack can threaten to kill 2 units. Lion allows you to clean up the board while also getting damage onto the opposing PB, and its 4-health means that it is more likely to survive long enough to be refreshed with Rejuv.

Even though the divine dice can be stretched relatively thin in this list, it is running both swordsmen since they work well with Lion to pressure guard. This does mean that some rounds you will need to choose between summoning a Lion or playing Sonic. If you anticipate knight level removal it may be best to bait it out with Lion, to preserve your limited number of Sonics.
