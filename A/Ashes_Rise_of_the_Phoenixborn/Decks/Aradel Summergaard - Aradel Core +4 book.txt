Built using only the master set and first 4 expansions. Some first five options:

Butterfly, Owl, Bear, Gilder, Widows  I’d use this as the “standard” first 5, as it will take some pressure off of your ceremonial dice in later rounds by taking a Widows out of the draw pile.

Jaguar, Owl, Bear, Gilder, Fester / Ice Trap / Choke  this one might be better against someone you expect to open with a Hammer Knight, especially if you expect Root Armor on that Knight, as you can Water Blast + Fester to take it out. Ice Trap would be good if you’re expecting something like False Demon in the open. Choke maybe against Brennan, Maeoni, Noah or other Aradels.

Jaguar, Bear, Gilder, Butterfly, Violinist  this is a 3rd option that might be cool if you expect a swarm of little guys.
