I played this deck in the first round of the top cut of Ashes International League 4, and I played it again in the single-deck shufflebus tournament that followed.

The basic concept of the deck is that the combination of Jaguars, Mind Fog Owls, and Frostback Bears can do a lot of bypass damage. Massive Growth gives you a powerful wincon, as it allows you to either remove big blockers with Seeds/Close Combat or swing around them using Jaguar Gaze, while Terrify and Unseen allow you to get past smaller blockers.

It's a very strong deck, albeit one that can struggle to find a plan B when the opponent has a good response to plan A. Still, I enjoy that a 2-color deck can still be competitive in the Reborn meta.

Typical first 5 is Jaguar, Bear, Gilder, Three-Eyed Owl, and Raptor Herder. The Three-Eyed Owl is great for providing early hand pressure on an opponent's first 5, but often gets meditated out when you draw into Mind Fog Owl.

My most likely edits to this deck would be to add a second raptor herder and probably a refresh, probably at the expense of the Sympathy Pains.
