Idea: Create a beefy unblockable mind fog owl, swing for big damage.
First Five: All Ready Spells & Either Raptor Herder if you will need early blockers or massive growth to put through big damage 1st turn.

Play down Owl & Other unit. Beef up owl with Massive Growth/Exhortation/Hunt Master. Use Hypnotize on non-owl unit, then swing in with owl & unblockable unit.

Crickets used as chump blocker/dice fixing. Also to get massive growth/allies back from discard.

Molten Gold to finish out games, or to kill a big unit when necessary.
