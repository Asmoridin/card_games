This is part of a series I’m building using only the Master Set and the Deluxe expansions (The Law of Lions, The Song of Soaksend and The Breaker of Fate).

This is a basic 4-book Aradel list designed to control the battlefield with your efficient conjurations, getting value round over round with your 4 summon books consistently pumping out units, and Water Blast giving you great control. Gilder + Water Blast can do 3 damage to something in one turn, which is great, and Anchornaut, Violinist or even a frog ping can finish off a 4-life knight. Lioness is also an excellent control tool to remove a problematic unit and get around unit or Phoenixborn guards.

Jaguar + Massive Growth + Crescendo can allow you to get through with big damage once you’ve worked the board down with your control tools. Flute Mage can even give you abother swing with the Massive unit if you can set it up. Also, Jaguar’s 2-basic cost will be big for this deck in making sure you always have a way to spend your 10 dice each round.

Redirect is a good spell to both protect Aradel’s low life total and to clear Anchornauts off of your board that are just taking up space. But don’t forget you can Power Through or even Massive Growth those Anchornauts to make them attackers!

FF suggestion:
Gilder, Lioness, Salamander, Jaguar, Iron Worker. That leaves you a die to water blast something. Just put out 5 good bodies and setup your spellboard for the rest of the game.
